one risk register for the whole project (business, technical, and team risks together), ranked, with owners, and listing every external prerequisite you need (an approval, an account, a review, recruitment) with who asked for it and when. 
This is the only risk register; do not maintain copies elsewhere. Your partner and the next team read it, so write each risk as a fact about the project, without blame (“partner review takes two weeks; we plan around it”). 
A problem between teammates goes to your Teaching Assistant (TA), not into the register. The Project Risks guide <https://capstone.alexulbrich.com/guides/risks/> shows how to write and rank each entry.
