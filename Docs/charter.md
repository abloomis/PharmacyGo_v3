Roles
Project Manager - Zach
Oversees communication with the project partner and instructors, leads team sprints and establishes goals and deadlines.
AI Coordinator - Nathan
Responsible for the AI context file, answers team questions related to what can and cannot be seen or edited by AI. 
Quality Owner - Shawn
Oversees the team’s building and testing pipeline, establishes the Definition of Done for deliverables, keeps repositories updated. 
Designer and Document Currency - Alex
Oversees and vets game design and art-direction decisions, keeps documents up-to-date.




Roles will rotate three times during the school year, giving each team member the opportunity to work in each role. Time on each role should be spent roughly equally. Therefore, the first rotation will occur during week 8 of fall term. The second rotation will occur during week 5 of winter term. Then the final rotation will occur during week 2 of spring term. 

Decision Process
Choices will be decided via team meetings, which can occur either during class on Friday mornings, on campus outside of class, or asynchronously through Discord or Zoom. For both minor decisions and major proposals, team members will put forward clear suggestions along with specific ways to implement the suggestions, then the remaining three team members will vote on the proposal. Proposals that pass with a majority of votes (2 out of 3) will be recorded and documented, with major non-reversible decisions being maintained in a team Architecture Decision Record (ADR).
Cadence and Tools
We plan to communicate primarily via Discord in a private server, as well as via our Outlook emails. Team meetings, when they need to occur, can be hosted either during (or right after) lectures on Friday mornings, or on campus on Tuesdays or Thursdays, as those are the days during which all four of us are most likely to be available. 

Our core tools for project planning and management will be GitHub and Google Docs. Quick notes can be written in shared Google Docs for easy access, while more formal team decisions and documents can be saved in our shared repository on GitHub as easily searchable markdown files. Key, non-reversible decisions will be voted upon and recorded explicitly in our team ADR, as mentioned above.
Conflict and Accountability
Our plan to address conflicts is to refer to our Team Working Agreement. 

Triggers: Any one of the following starts the steps below:
A teammate misses two team meetings in a row without notifying the team beforehand. 
A task passes its deadline by more than 3 days without a status update.

Anyone who notices a trigger or a problem must post it in our team Discord within 2 days. 
The people involved must talk one-on-one within 3 days of the post and agree on a fix. 
If the problem is not resolved 3 days after that conversation, we discuss it at the next team meeting and record an action plan with an owner and a date in our shared notes. 
If the problem continues 7 days after the action plan was recorded, the PM emails the instruction team, or the Quality Owner does if the PM is involved. 

Contribution is judged based on pull requests, reviews, and meeting attendance records, not from recollection or anecdotal testimony.
Inclusion Norms
We all attend the Corvallis campus and can meet physically every week. We all operate on the Pacific timezone, and can each contribute to note-taking and documentation by using shared Google Docs and shared repositories. Any time a team member is unable to attend a meeting physically, they can try to join asynchronously, or alternatively ask for a meeting recap. 
AI Confidentiality
Intended Use: We will be using AI such as Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini to assist in coding the project’s functionalities and systems as well as brainstorming ideas and concepts.
Personal Data: Any confidential/personal information provided to the AI tool WILL NOT be used to train, retrain, or fine-tune AI models, private or public.
Informed Consent: User provided information will only be used for what the project is intended for, and informed consent will be obtained from users in an explicit and clear manner. Users can revoke consent or delete their shared data at any given time.
Human Review: All AI-assisted code and generated content will be human reviewed and verified extensively before being finalized, shared, and used.
